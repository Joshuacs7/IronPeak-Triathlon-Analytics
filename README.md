# IronPeak Full Distance Triathlon — Performance Analytics

## From Race Data to Business Decisions

An end-to-end analysis of the 2019 IronPeak Full Distance Triathlon focused on completion, participant performance, country-level participation, and division-level differences.

The goal was to identify meaningful performance patterns and translate them into actionable recommendations for future event planning.

## The Business Question

IronPeak needed to understand more than how many athletes completed the event.

The analysis focused on three questions:

- How strong was overall event completion?
- How did performance differ across athlete segments, countries, and divisions?
- What patterns could inform future participant strategy and event planning?

The objective was to move from **aggregate event metrics to actionable performance insights**.

---

## What the Data Revealed

### 01 — Overall completion was strong

**92.77% completion rate.** 2,258 of 2,434 participants finished the race, providing a strong baseline for evaluating variation across participant segments.

---

### 02 — Professional athletes showed a different risk profile

**18.75% professional DNF vs. 3.42% among amateurs.** Professionals averaged 8:59 versus 11:31 for amateurs, indicating a materially different completion and performance profile.

---

### 03 — Participation volume did not consistently align with performance

**USA: 650 participants vs. Germany: 277.** The largest participant market did not have the strongest average performance, highlighting a distinction between market size and athlete performance.

**Participation volume:** The USA was the largest market.

## Recommendations

### 01 — Review Professional Athlete Performance Separately

Analyze professional athletes separately to identify patterns associated with their substantially higher DNF rate.

### 02 — Combine Market Size With Performance

Use both participation volume and average performance when evaluating country-level opportunities and future strategies.

### 03 — Use Division-Level Performance Benchmarks

Incorporate division-level benchmarks when evaluating overall performance and planning future participant strategies.

## Scope & Methodology

**Scope:** Single-event descriptive analysis based on available participant and race data, focused on demographics, race performance, completion, and segmentation patterns.

**Approach:** Data preparation → descriptive analysis → segmentation → performance comparison → business interpretation.

The analysis identifies observed patterns within the available data and is not intended to establish causal relationships.
